
Before any real details can be shared, NDA's must be signed

Unilateral NDA - One party must maintain confidentiality, the other is free to discuss details
Bilateral NDA - Both parties must maintain confidentiality about the operation
Multilateral NDA - A bilateral NDA with more than two parties

Make sure the person requesting the pen test ACTUALLY HAS THIS AUTHORITY!!!

# Scoping Questionnaire

- Clarifies the type of service they are seeking (internal, external, wireless, physical, web application, social engineering)
- Black box, semi invasive etc
- What is out of scope
- Active Directory assessment
- Physical locations geographically sparse etc

# Pre-engagement meeting

- May walk through and review the scoping questionnaires
- Like visiting the doctors before an operation to understand what will be performed and how to expect the procedure to go, what it will do etc.
- May at this point include time/cost estimations
- Risks
- Contact information for the company and an escalative priority order
- Serve the steak as they ordered it

# Contract 
- Developed with the client throughout the pre-engagement meeting


# Rules of Engagement

- Goals, scope, time of day to test, evidence handling, objectives/flags, system backups, incident reporting, permission to test signed.

# Kick off meeting

- Explain the handling of external critical vulnerability discovery (SQL injection, remote code execution) to determine an emergency contact.
- Internal test alerts only usually occur if a system becomes unresponsive, illegal activity is found or there is evidence of an external threat actor in the network or a prior breach
- Brute forcing may lock users out, and the test may trigger internal security alarms. We should be contacted immediately if the test negatively impacts their network
- Determine how many other pentests the client has had, influencing the length of this initial meeting

# Contractors agreement

- "Get out of Jail free card" in the event we are physically on a site and a security-aware employee alerts the police
- Must detail physical addresses, building names, components, establish a timeline and the floors of the building used.
