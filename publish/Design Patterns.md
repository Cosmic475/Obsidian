A design pattern is a blueprint for an Object Oriented structure which handles a recurring software problem. They ease communication between programmers and may enhance the pace at which developers can interpret a piece of software

There are three categories of patterns:
- Creational
- Structural
- Behavioural


## Structural Patterns

> Goal - Compose classes and objects to form large, flexible (loosely coupled) structures
> Fingerprint - multiple conditional statements.


### Examples
- Adapter - allowing two classes with incompatible interfaces work together by translation
