At the proof-of-concept stage we condense our findings into neat demonstrations of the vulnerability to our customer. This may be by way of screenshot or ideally a script which can be handed to the network administrators for testing. However, we need to make clear that this script is only one way of exploiting the vulnerability; fighting to "patch" the script may still leave other bad actors with methods of exploitation.
- Breaking a segment of the attack chain does not suffice fixing the vulnerability
